PROJECT VYAPAAR: BUSINESS DATA TREASURE HUNT

Scenario:
Vyapaar Retail is an Indian omnichannel retailer. Revenue appears healthy, but management is concerned about profitability, customer experience, logistics and marketing efficiency. Analyse the six datasets and present evidence-based recommendations.

Rules:
1. Document every cleaning decision.
2. Do not delete unusual values without investigation.
3. Join tables using the ID fields.
4. Separate facts from assumptions.
5. Final submission: cleaned data/model, dashboard or notebook, and 3-minute boardroom pitch.

Core missions:
- Identify and correct data-quality problems.
- Find the strongest and weakest product/category performance.
- Investigate discounting and profitability.
- Identify high-value and repeat customers.
- Diagnose delivery and rating problems.
- Evaluate marketing efficiency.
- Find one significant insight not explicitly requested.
