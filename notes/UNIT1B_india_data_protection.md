# UNIT 1B — INDIA: PUTTASWAMY → SRIKRISHNA → JPC → DPDP 2022 → DPDP ACT 2023
*(† = general knowledge, not in readings.)*

## 0. Big picture
| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 2000/2011 | IT Act **s 43A** + **SPDI Rules 2011**† |
| Aug 2017 | **Srikrishna Committee** constituted (MeitY memo 31 Jul 2017) |
| **24 Aug 2017** | ***Puttaswamy***: 9-judge bench, **privacy fundamental right** |
| **27 Jul 2018** | Srikrishna report **"A Free and Fair Digital Economy"** + Draft PDP Bill 2018 |
| Dec 2019 | PDP Bill 2019 → JPC† |
| **16 Dec 2021** | **JPC report** → DP Bill 2021 |
| Aug 2022 | 2021 Bill withdrawn† |
| **18 Nov 2022** | Draft DPDP Bill 2022† |
| **3 Aug 2023** | DPDP Bill 2023 (No. 113)†; Act assent **11 Aug 2023** (No. 22)† |
| Nov 2025 | DPDP Rules 2025†; substantive duties from 13 May 2027 (per Unit 3 notes) |

**One-sentence story:** *Puttaswamy* → State must build a DP law. **2018**: rights-heavy GDPR-like **fiduciary** model + strong regulator. **2021 JPC**: sovereignty/national security. **2022**: slim, business-friendly, **executive-controlled**. **2023**: tidier (legitimate uses, statutory Board, blacklist) but **broad State exemptions**, **RTI diluted**.
**4 themes:** (1) who's protected against whom (State exemptions: 2018 s 42 → cl 35 → 2022 s 18 → 2023 s 17); (2) basis for processing (consent → non-consent grounds → deemed consent → legitimate uses); (3) enforcer (independent DPA → govt Board); (4) data flows (mirroring → whitelist → blacklist).
**Terminology:** Data Principal = data subject · Fiduciary = controller · Processor · SDF (risk-based duties) · DPA/Board = supervisory authority · Consent Manager (none) · Sensitive/critical data = Art 9.

## 1. PUTTASWAMY (2017) 10 SCC 1 — 24 Aug 2017, 9 judges
Khehar CJ, Chelameswar, Bobde, Agrawal, Nariman, Sapre, **Chandrachud**, Kaul, Nazeer.
**Bottom line:** unanimous: privacy = fundamental right, intrinsic to **Art 21** + **Part III**. *M.P. Sharma* (1954) and *Kharak Singh* (1964) majority **overruled**. Covers **informational privacy**; **State and non-State actors** (Kaul); **not absolute** — **legality, legitimate aim, proportionality**.
**Reference:** began as **Aadhaar** challenge; 3-judge bench doubted (M.P. Sharma 8 judges, Kharak Singh 6); referring order 11 Aug 2015 ("denuded of vigour and vitality"). Chandrachud: "left alone in a core which is inviolable"; liberty "in an interconnected world".
**Arguments:** Union (larger benches bind); **Haryana** (right "conceptually unsound"; only **DP legislation** can address); Centre for Civil Society (undefinable); **Petitioners**: "left alone" under-inclusive; inner sphere vs State and non-State; **4-part ECtHR test: sanctioned by law; necessary in democratic society for legitimate aim; proportionate; procedural guarantees** (= ancestor of proportionality in *Puttaswamy II* 2018 and *Anuradha Bhasin* 2020). **Irony:** State conceded DP law needed.
**Six opinions:**
- **Chandrachud (plurality, + Khehar, Agrawal, Nazeer):** Gopalan "silos" overruled by *R.C. Cooper*/*Maneka Gandhi*; liberty and dignity; effect not object. **Para 66 (seven *Canara Bank* points):** privacy from Arts 19+21; grounded in *Kharak Singh*, *Gobind*, UDHR/ICCPR; **attaches to the person**; safeguards vs arbitrary search; Collector can't delegate; **reasonable expectation info used only for purpose** (purpose limitation); State may need data but no private entity access. † three-fold test (legality, legitimate aim, proportionality); overruled *ADM Jabalpur*.
- **Chelameswar:** tiered scrutiny — **repose, sanctuary, intimate decision**; "Definitional uncertainty is no reason…"; para 45 compelling State interest for strictest claims, else **just, fair and reasonable**.
- **Bobde:** precondition of freedoms; para 34 Arts 19(1), 20(3), 25, 28, 29; **future technology**; para 47 inalienable, Part III; Art 12.
- **Nariman:** 121 pages; para 54 three intrusions (**body, information, choices**); critique of *US v Miller*; Tribe "informational autonomy"; Right to Financial Privacy Act 1978; 2013 Necessary & Proportionate Principles; para 94.
- **Sapre†:** natural right, dignity, fraternity.
- **Kaul (technology):** para 12 "qua State and non-State actors"; para 13 profiling (GDPR Art 4(4)), Snowden; paras 16–19 **Uber/Facebook/Alibaba/Airbnb**, passive data, **"Knowledge about a person gives a power over that person"**, stultifying effect on dissent; para 20 BlackBerry; para 22 Orwell; para 69 RTBF; para 75 "**So there is hope!**" (Srikrishna); **para 77** inner sphere.
**Holding†:** M.P. Sharma overruled; Kharak Singh majority overruled; privacy = Art 21 + Part III; *Gobind*, *Canara Bank* correct. **Why it matters:** foundation for all bills; purpose limitation & proportionality as benchmarks for State exemptions; horizontal reach; India has no separate DP right (privacy does the work).

## 2. SRIKRISHNA COMMITTEE (PRS summary, 1 Aug 2018)
**Fiduciary relationship** (individual depends → fair, authorised purposes). Duties: **fair and reasonable** processing; **notice**. **Sensitive data** (caste, religion, sexual orientation) → **explicit consent**. **Consent** precondition, informed; extra for children. **4 non-consensual grounds:** State welfare; law/court orders; prompt action; employment. **3 participation rights groups:** access/confirmation/correction; object/ADM/direct marketing + portability; RTBF. Independent **DPA**; **SDFs**. Law sets minimum standards; amend Aadhaar Act. (Draft Bill omitted general object/ADM rights.) **Why "fiduciary":** relationship of trust (Balkin†); consent central but limited; label survived into 2023, **substantive duties of loyalty did not**.

## 3. DRAFT PDP BILL 2018 (112 sections, 15 chapters)
**Bottom line:** GDPR-style; principles; **six grounds**; sensitive data; children; rights; accountability (**data trust scores**); **localisation**; DPA + adjudication wing; **4% fines**; compensation; **criminal offences**; State exemptions need **law + necessity + proportionality**.
- **Scope s 2:** extraterritorial (business in India / offering goods/services / **profiling**); anonymised excluded. **Definitions:** fiduciary incl. **State**; **child <18**; **Harm s 3(21) — 10 heads** (bodily/mental; identity; financial; reputation; employment; **discrimination**; blackmail; denial from **evaluative decision**; **fear of being observed**; **unexpected surveillance**); **SPD s 3(35) — 13 heads** (passwords, financial, health, official ID/Aadhaar, sex life, orientation, biometric, genetic, transgender, intersex, caste/tribe, religious/political belief, Authority-specified).
- **Obligations:** s 4 fair & reasonable · s 5 purpose limitation · s 6 collection limitation · s 7 lawful · s 8 notice (~12 items incl. **trust score**) · s 9 quality · s 10 storage · s 11 accountability.
- **Grounds:** consent s 12 (free/informed/specific/clear/withdrawable; **no conditionality** s 12(3); **burden on fiduciary**); State functions s 13; law/court s 14; prompt action s 15; employment s 16; **reasonable purposes s 17** (Authority-specified: fraud, whistle-blowing, M&A, security, credit scoring, debt recovery, public data).
- **SPD (ss 18–22):** **explicit consent**; State functions only "**strictly necessary**" (services/benefits, **not** licences); prompt action limited.
- **Children s 23:** under 18; age verification + parental consent; **guardian data fiduciaries** barred from profiling/tracking/targeted ads.
- **Rights:** access (summary) · correction · **portability** (incl. profiles) · **RTBF = restrict disclosure** via Adjudicating Officer (weigh free speech) · **no general object/ADM/erasure**.
- **Accountability:** privacy by design; transparency; security; **breach → Authority if likely harm** (Authority decides whether to tell users); **DPIA**; records (State too); **annual audits**; **DPO**; processors via valid contract; **SDFs**; grievance 30 days.
- **Cross-border:** s 40(1) **mirror copy** in India; s 40(2) **critical data only in India**; s 41 SCCs/adequacy/necessity (+ explicit consent for SPD).
- **Exemptions:** security of State s 42 (**Parliamentary law + necessary + proportionate**); crime s 43; legal proceedings s 44; research s 45; domestic s 46; **journalism s 47**; small entities s 48 (≤₹20 lakh; ≤100 principals/day).
- **DPA:** chair + 6; **CJI-led selection**; 5-year term, no reappointment, 2-year cooling-off; codes of practice; actions incl. suspend business/cross-border flows; **Adjudicating Officers**.
- **Penalties:** **₹5 cr/2%** and **₹15 cr/4%** worldwide turnover; **compensation s 75** (class complaints; joint & several); Appellate Tribunal → SC. **Offences:** 3 yrs/₹2 lakh; SPD 5 yrs/₹3 lakh; re-identification; cognizable, non-bailable.
- **Misc:** **s 98** binding policy directions (independence concern); **s 43A deleted**; **RTI s 8(1)(j)** amended keeping harm-vs-public-interest.
- **Strengths/weaknesses:** comprehensive, Puttaswamy-style limits vs broad State function ground, no object/erasure/ADM, localisation cost, s 98.

## 4. JPC REPORT & DP BILL 2021
JPC chair **P.P. Chaudhary**; tabled **16 Dec 2021**; >2 yrs. Frame: **data as national asset** (AatmaNirbhar), national security; GDPR-compatibility. Primer: report "sets context and offers justification" for amendments.
**Recs:** **1** one law for **personal + non-personal** (can't separate); **2** 24-month phase; **5** non-intermediary social media = **publishers**; **8** IoT certification; **10** **mirror copy** of SPD/critical data held abroad; **11** localisation policy (infrastructure, payment systems, start-ups, **tax data flows**, local AI); **38** **72-hour breach**.
**Changes 2019→2021:** **digital-only** (para 2.12); NPD in scope; **criminal penalties** (≤3 yrs/₹2 lakh; only on Authority's complaint; directors only if knowledge); localisation (SPD stored in India; **critical only in India**; DPA approval); accountability (**FAT of algorithms**, DPOs, PbD); social media (verify users; parent office in India; statutory media regulator); 24 months; no localisation timeline.
**Critique:** scope creep; digital-only narrower than GDPR; sovereignty/protectionism; criminalisation (dropped later); social media mixes content regulation; **cl 35 exemption retained** (dissent: Ramesh, O'Brien, Tewari†). Positives: 72h, DPO, PbD, FAT.

## 5. DPDP BILL 2022 (30 sections, 6 chapters, 1 Schedule)
**Bottom line:** radical slim-down. **Drops** SPD, critical data, localisation, NPD, criminal offences, independent DPA, portability, RTBF, harm architecture. **Introduces** **deemed consent**, **duties of Data Principals**, govt-designed **Board**, **whitelist**, **civil penalties ≤₹500 cr**, "her/she", digital-by-design Board. Heavy delegation + broad State exemptions. **Purpose:** balance right vs need to process.
- **Definitions:** harm only **4 heads**; person incl. State; personal data "identifiable by or in relation to"; breach (CIA); processing = automated, digital; **public interest** incl. "**false statements of fact**" (novel/controversial). **s 3:** Rules = Act; 22 languages; "her/she". **s 4:** digital data (online / offline-digitised); extraterritorial **offering or profiling**; excluded: non-automated, offline, domestic, **≥100-year records**.
- **s 5:** "lawful purpose" = **not expressly forbidden**; only **consent** or **deemed consent**. **s 6 notice:** itemised **data + purpose only** (thin vs 2018).
- **s 7 consent:** free/specific/informed/unambiguous; invalid parts void; withdraw any time **as easy**, "consequences borne by principal"; processors stop; **Consent Managers** (DEPA/AA); **no bundling** (concluded contract); burden on fiduciary.
- **s 8 DEEMED CONSENT (9 situations):** voluntary + reasonably expected; **State functions "any function under any law"**; judgments/orders; medical emergency; epidemic; disaster; **employment** (incl. biometric attendance); "**public interest**" (fraud, M&A, security, **credit scoring**, search engines, public data, debt recovery); **fair & reasonable prescribed purposes** (≈ legit interests). **Critique:** legal fiction; consent rights confusion; too broad for State; no balancing.
- **s 9:** accountability **"irrespective of any agreement"**; accuracy (reasonable efforts); security **₹250 cr**; **breach to Board AND each principal, no threshold/timeline** ₹200 cr; storage limit (**"legal or business purposes" loophole**); valid contract/sub-processors.
- **s 10 children:** verifiable parental consent; no harm; **no tracking/behavioural monitoring/targeted ads**; ₹200 cr.
- **s 11 SDF:** Central Govt notifies; factors incl. electoral democracy; DPO in India, auditor, DPIA; ₹150 cr.
- **Rights ss 12–16:** information ("in one place"), correction & **erasure** (new), grievance (7 days, exhaust first), **nomination**, **s 16 DUTIES** (₹10,000; costs on complainants) — **inverts rights framework**. **Absent:** portability, RTBF, object, ADM, restriction.
- **s 17 transfer = WHITELIST**; no criteria; no localisation. **s 18 exemptions:** (1) legal claims/courts/**crime ("any contravention of any law")**/outsourcing (security s 9(4) kept); (2) **Central Govt notification** exempts State instrumentalities from **any/all** provisions (no law, no proportionality) vs 2018 s 42; (3) classes (start-ups); (4) **State exempt from storage limitation**. **No journalism exemption.**
- **Board ss 19–25:** by Central Govt notification; composition by Rules; **adjudicatory only**; no suo motu; can't seize equipment affecting day-to-day business; appeal **High Court 60 days**; ADR; **voluntary undertaking** (bars proceedings); **₹500 cr per instance**, **no compensation**; Schedule: ₹250/200/200/150 cr, ₹10,000, ₹50 cr.
- **Misc:** Rules; Schedule cap 2×; removal of difficulties **5 yrs**; **s 43A omitted**; **RTI 8(1)(j)** override + proviso deleted.
- **Criticisms (12):** deemed consent; executive dominance (~20+ "as may be prescribed"); State exemptions; no SPD; reduced rights + duties; no compensation; RTI; narrow harm; breach notification fatigue; "false statements" censorship; weak transfer criteria; no independent regulator.

## 6. DPDP BILL 2023 → ACT (44 clauses, 9 chapters)
**Bottom line:** keeps 2022 skeleton; **legitimate uses** replace deemed consent (public interest & "fair and reasonable" dropped; some become exemptions); **blacklist**; **public data excluded** (s 3(c)(ii)); statutory Board (govt-appointed, **2-yr** terms); appeals to **TDSAT**; **blocking (s 37)** + **call for information (s 36)**; "verifiably safe" age relaxation (s 9(5)); start-up exemption (17(3)); **5-yr exempt-anyone (17(5))**; **RTI gutted**. Introduced by **Ashwini Vaishnaw**; passed by **voice vote, no committee**†.
- **Definitions:** TDSAT; Consent Manager **registered with Board**; principal incl. **guardian of person with disability**; processing "wholly or partly" automated; State = Art 12; **"harm" & "public interest" removed**. **Cl 3:** extraterritorial **goods/services only** ("profiling" dropped); excludes **personal/domestic** and **publicly available data** (by principal or legal obligation) → **scraping/AI training**.
- **Cl 4–6:** consent or legitimate uses. **Cl 5 notice** + how to exercise rights/complain; **legacy consent continues until withdrawal**. **Cl 6 consent:** "free, specific, informed, **unconditional**, unambiguous"; **limited to necessary data** (telemedicine illustration); consequences borne by principal (e-commerce illustration).
- **Cl 7 LEGITIMATE USES:** (a) voluntary + no objection; (b) State benefits (prior consent or notified database; maternity illustration = **purpose creep**); (c) State functions/sovereignty; (d) legal disclosure to State; (e) judgments (incl. **foreign civil**); (f) medical emergency; (g) epidemic; (h) disaster/public order; (i) employment. **No general legitimate-interests ground.**
- **Cl 8:** accountability; valid contract; accuracy; **security ₹250 cr**; **breach intimation ₹200 cr** (no threshold/timeline); **erasure on withdrawal / purpose served unless law** (business loophole removed; **inactivity lapse**).
- **Cl 9:** parent/guardian consent; "detrimental effect on well-being"; **no tracking/targeted ads**; **(5) "verifiably safe" age relaxation**; ₹200 cr. **Cl 10 SDF:** ₹150 cr.
- **Rights:** **Cl 11 access** (summary + recipients **and processors**) **only vs consent-based processing**; law-enforcement sharing hidden. **Cl 12** correction/erasure (State exempt). **Cl 13** grievance (exhaust first). **Cl 14** nominate. **Cl 15** duties ₹10,000.
- **Cl 16 BLACKLIST** (default open); **sectoral localisation survives (RBI)**.
- **Cl 17:** (1) adds **regulators**, **M&A schemes**, **loan defaulters**; **(2)(a) Act does not apply at all** to notified State instrumentalities (and Centre's processing of their data); (3) start-ups; (4) State exempt from erasure; **(5) 5-yr power to exempt any fiduciary from any provision**.
- **Board cls 18–28:** Chair + members as Centre notifies; **appointed by Centre**; ≥1 legal expert; **2 years, re-appointable**; removal by Centre ("moral turpitude in opinion of Central Government"); 1-yr cooling-off; staff need approval; adjudicatory only.
- **Cl 29 TDSAT** 60 days / 6 months → SC; **cl 32** undertaking; **cl 33–34** penalties to Consolidated Fund, **no compensation**; Schedule (₹250/200/200/150 cr; ₹10,000; undertaking breach; ₹50 cr); cl 42 cap 2×.
- **Cl 35–44:** immunity; **36 call for information** (Notes on Clauses misdescribe); **37 blocking** after penalties in **2+ instances** (≈ IT Act 69A); 39 civil courts barred; **40: 26 rule-making matters**; removal of difficulties **3 yrs**; **44(2) s 43A omitted**; **44(3) RTI s 8(1)(j) → "information which relates to personal information"**. Budget **₹25 cr + ₹10 cr/yr**.

**FOR:** SARAL; Puttaswamy mandate; GDPR-like consent; Consent Managers; child protection; high penalties; pro-innovation; honest relabelling; Art 19(2) grounds, judicial review. **AGAINST:** executive overreach; fails proportionality; weak rights/duties on individuals; no SPD; public data excluded; RTI/journalism; weak Board/TDSAT/budget; no harm approach; children age-gating; no compensation; process (no JPC, voice vote); extraterritorial gap.

**The arc:** rights-based (2018) → sovereignist (2019–21) → minimalist (2022) → pragmatic consolidation (2023); constant = **broad State exemptions** vs proportionality.
**13 DPDP vs GDPR differences:** legal bases; SPD; rights only consent-based/no portability, objection, restriction, ADM; **duties on individuals**; regulator independence (Art 52); penalties fixed vs %; compensation; public data; transfers (blacklist vs adequacy); State exemptions (legislation+proportionality vs notification); children (18, flat ban on ads/tracking); breach (all vs risk-based); Consent Managers.

## 8. Debates, exam Qs, cheat sheet
**Debates:** (1) consent vs fiduciary accountability; (2) State as fiduciary (Puttaswamy: legality, legitimate aim, proportionality, procedural safeguards; govt: Art 19(2), judicial review, IT Act s 69/Telegraph s 5; against: no surveillance reform, State largest processor, **s 7(b)–(c)** asymmetry); (3) localisation (for: MLATs, security, economy, sovereignty; against: cost, trade friction, splinternet, surveillance); (4) regulator independence; (5) delegated legislation; (6) RTI/free speech/no journalism exemption; (7) children (18 vs evolving capacities); (8) consent in India; (9) NPD.
**Likely Qs:** Puttaswamy→2023 · deemed consent vs legitimate uses · State exemptions vs Puttaswamy · 2018 vs 2023 regulators · localisation · DPDP vs GDPR · fiduciary vs notice-and-consent · children · Puttaswamy sources/limits · duties on individuals.
