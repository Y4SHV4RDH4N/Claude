## Last-look sheet (read this before walking in)

### Unit 1
- **Three theories:** W&B = "right to be let alone"/**inviolate personality** (tort; 6 limitations; **truth & malice no defence**) · Roessler = **autonomy**, **decisional/informational/local**, "dimensions not spaces", democracy · Solove = **16 harms/4 groups**, **family resemblance**, **secrecy paradigm**, **dignitary vs architectural**.
- **Traps:** W&B were lawyers not justices; "let alone" is Cooley's; Prosser made 4 torts; blackmail = power; exposure ≠ disclosure.
- **Puttaswamy:** 24 Aug 2017, 9 judges; Art 21 + Part III; overruled M.P. Sharma/Kharak Singh; **legality, legitimate aim, proportionality (+ procedural safeguards)**; Kaul: State **and non-State**.
- **India timeline:** Srikrishna (Jul 2018, fiduciary, 4 non-consent grounds) → JPC (16 Dec 2021, NPD, 72h, localisation) → 2022 (30 sections, **deemed consent**, whitelist, High Court, ₹500 cr, **duties on principals**) → 2023 (44 clauses, **legitimate uses**, **blacklist**, **TDSAT**, **17(2)(a)/17(5)**, **s 36/37**, **RTI**, public data excluded).
- **Always argue:** State exemptions vs Puttaswamy proportionality; no SPD; weak/captured Board; consent-centric despite "fiduciary" label.

### Unit 2
- **Thesis for the big essay:** notice-and-consent survives only if self-management cost falls and controllers are constrained *after* consent; **law + design + technology complementary, Art 25 GDPR the bridge.**
- ***Planet49*:** pre-ticked box ≠ consent; applies to non-personal data (device integrity); tell **duration + third-party access**; **bundling not decided**; critique "regulates the click".
- **Santos:** 3 of 10 Nielsen heuristics → 10 design choices; "Not yet" / right to be ambivalent; DPAs conflict on colours. **Pałka:** longer complete policies + LLMs; real policies answer 2/9; GPT-4 33/45. **Pilot:** formal policy language + SPIN. **Najdi:** don't hand data over; 9/10 rights; IR10 fails. **DPC:** Art 28(3).
- **McDonald & Cranor:** 244 h, $3,534, $781 bn. **Iwaya/Wairimu:** PIAs perfunctory; **validated ≠ evaluated**; 9/39 evaluated. **C&V:** rights don't reach inferences; only Arts 21–22. **Lindegren:** DAD +36 s, SUS 81, recall n.s. **Dove & Chen:** consent misconception; "South Africa+". **Medzini:** polycentric; DPO + educators. **Kay & Terry:** 7 s → 37 s; summaries displace reading.

### Unit 3
- **Test:** purposes + **essential means** (data type, duration, recipients, data subjects) = controller; non-essential means (software, security detail) may be processor's. **Functional not contractual.** Processor beyond instructions → **Art 28(10)** = controller.
- **Joint control:** common or **converging decisions**, "inextricably linked"; **no access needed; joint ≠ equal; only jointly determined phases** (*Wirtschaftsakademie* C-210/16, *Jehovah's Witnesses* C-25/17, *Fashion ID* C-40/17; *Google Spain* C-131/12). **Art 26(3):** rights against each. Mere commercial benefit ≠ joint control.
- **Art 28(3):** 8 clauses (a)–(h). **Sub-processors:** specific authorisation (silence = denied) vs general (silence = ok); Art 28(4) same obligations; initial processor liable.
- **Opinion 22/2024:** know the whole chain; verify (risk changes extent not existence); no duty to *systematically* collect sub-contracts; "practical difficulties don't exonerate"; "unless required by law" wording recommended not required, broad wording ≠ instruction.
- **Compliance gap (Baquero):** back-end blind spot; 4 defects (dispersed, static, convoluted, legalistic); Atos documents; Symboleo/nuXmv/Hyperledger; limit "garbage in, garbage out". **⚠ paper says "Art 7"; correct = Art 5(2)/24.**
- **India:** DPDP s 2(i)/(k); **s 8(1)** single accountability; **s 8(2)** valid contract (not s 8(6)); Rule 6(1)(f) only content rule; **no direct processor duties, no Art 28(10), no Art 26, no compensation**; *Dedalus* fine impossible; RBI NBFC outsourcing direction fills gap.
